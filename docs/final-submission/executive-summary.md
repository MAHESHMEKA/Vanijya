# Vanijya (वाणिज्य) — Executive Summary
**Smart India Hackathon 2024 — Problem Statement 26132**
**Theme:** Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development  
**Title:** Strengthening Market Linkages & Price Discovery for Farmers

---

## 1. Problem Context & The Agricultural Dilemma

In India, over **86% of farmers are smallholders** operating on less than 2 hectares of land. When harvest arrives, they face severe systemic challenges:
1. **Asymmetric Price Information:** Farmers rely on local agents (*arhtiyas*) for mandi price knowledge, often receiving below-market rates.
2. **Distress Selling:** Due to perishable produce and lack of storage intelligence, farmers sell immediately at bottom-cycle prices.
3. **High Intermediary Costs:** Middlemen commissions, unofficial weighment deductions, and transit delays consume 8% to 15% of the crop's realized value.
4. **Buyer Inefficiencies:** Institutional food processors and wholesale buyers struggle to find direct, quality-graded farm produce with origin traceability.

---

## 2. The Solution: Vanijya (वाणिज्य)

**Vanijya** is an end-to-end digital marketplace and decision intelligence platform that links farmers directly with verified buyers while providing real-time price discovery:

- **Mandi Intelligence & Spatial Arbitrage:** Pulls benchmark rates from Agmarknet / data.gov.in, computes 7-day Simple Moving Averages, and uses the Haversine formula to identify high-return nearby mandis after transport deductions.
- **Rule-Based Best Selling Window:** An explainable advisory engine that recommends optimal selling timeframes (e.g. *Next 24–48 Hours*) without black-box ML risks.
- **Farm-Gate Listing & Direct Bidding:** Farmers publish crop lots with quality grading; verified buyers submit transparent digital bids.
- **Atomic Deal Finalization:** With 1 tap, the farmer accepts the winning offer, automatically generating a legal transaction contract and digital payment invoice.
- **0% Intermediary Commission:** 100% of the agreed transaction value flows directly to the farmer.

---

## 3. Measurable Impact Metrics

- **+11.4% Net Income Increase:** Achieved through spatial arbitrage discovery and elimination of middleman cuts.
- **₹19,125 Saved per 100 Qtl Batch:** Zero intermediary commissions and unrecorded deductions.
- **<3 Minute Full Sourcing Loop:** From price check to verified payout.
